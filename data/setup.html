1. Calibrate the distance sensor

The VCNL4200 measures reflected light, not distance. Aim it at the salt (or a white card) at 3-5 known distances across your tank's range, measure each with a tape, and capture it. Optional first step: point it at nothing within 1.5 m and capture a baseline.

#DistanceRaw counts

2. Tank marks

Distances from the sensor face. Empty is the lowest useful salt level (tank floor or grid plate); Full is the salt surface when you've just filled it. Type a value or use the live reading.

3. Measurement

Changing LED current or integration time changes the raw counts — recalibrate afterwards.

4. Notifications

Alerts always appear on this dashboard. For phone push, install the ntfy app and subscribe to a hard-to-guess topic name.
